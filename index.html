Privacy Policy — StoryTime

Effective date: October 2026

StoryTime ("the app") is a children's story reading application. This page describes our privacy practices — in short, we collect nothing.

Information We Do Not Collect

StoryTime does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or sign-ups are required.
  • No names, email addresses, or contact details are collected.
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting data is sent anywhere.
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.
  • No location data.
  • No camera access.

Microphone and Voice Recordings

The app can record a parent or child reading a story aloud, so the story can be played back in their own voice. The microphone is only used while a recording is in progress, after the user taps the record button and grants permission. Recordings are saved only on the device. They are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere, and they can be deleted at any time from the story screen. Uninstalling the app also deletes them.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android's standard storage. This includes:

  • Selected language (English, Spanish, or Portuguese)
  • Font size, text style, and narration speed preferences
  • Autoplay and sleep timer preferences
  • Favorite stories and stories already read
  • Whether the full version has been unlocked

This data never leaves your device. It is only used to remember your settings between sessions.

In-App Purchase

StoryTime includes a free trial with some of its stories. The rest of the stories and the voice recording feature can be unlocked with a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. StoryTime never sees or stores payment details; it only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made. Purchases require an adult to answer a question before continuing.

Internet Access

All stories, narrations, illustrations, and content are bundled with the app at installation, so reading and listening work offline. The app only connects to Google Play to make or restore a purchase.

Children's Privacy

StoryTime is designed for use by young children under parental supervision. Because the app collects no personal data, it is compliant with children's privacy regulations including COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU).

Third-Party Services

StoryTime does not integrate any analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms. The only third-party service used is Google Play Billing, to process the optional purchase described above.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated policy will be made available at the same URL and the effective date will be updated.
